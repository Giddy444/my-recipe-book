# My Recipe Book V8

Mobile-first recipe book for Lithuanian, Belarusian, Spanish and English recipes.

## V8 highlights
- 4 country collections
- 40+ everyday/traditional recipes
- 🏺 Heritage Recipes collection
- Historical/traditional classification labels
- Short history notes for heritage dishes
- Weekly breakfast/lunch/dinner planner
- Nutrition estimates per portion
- Shopping list and route planning
- Supermarket selector and comparison demo
- Favourites and custom recipes stored locally

## Heritage recipe policy
Historical recipes are labelled as either:
- Traditional recipe
- Historical reconstruction
- Historical-inspired adaptation

The app avoids presenting modern reconstructions as exact ancient recipes. Historical recipes are simplified for modern home cooking and should be treated as reconstructions unless a source provides a directly documented recipe.

## Important
Supermarket prices and aisle guides in this prototype are demonstration data, not live quotations. Exact nutrition and supermarket availability can vary.
