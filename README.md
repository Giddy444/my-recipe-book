# My Recipe Book V8

A mobile-first recipe book for Lithuanian, Belarusian, Spanish and English cooking.

## V8
- 40 recipes — 10 per country
- Country → category → recipe navigation
- 🏺 Heritage recipes and historical/traditional labels
- ➕ Add any recipe to the weekly meal plan
- Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner planning for Monday–Sunday
- 👨‍🍳 Cook Mode with step-by-step navigation
- ⭐ Favourites
- 📝 Add your own recipes
- 🔥 Estimated nutrition per portion
- 🛒 Shopping list generated from the weekly plan
- 🗺️ Shopping route planning
- Supermarket selector and comparison demo

Sunday Roast, Cottage Pie, Fish & Chips, Shepherd’s Pie, Fish Pie, Toad in the Hole, Lancashire Hotpot, Sticky Toffee Pudding and heritage recipes.

## Heritage recipes

Heritage dishes are clearly labelled as:
- Traditional recipe
- Historical reconstruction
- Historical-inspired adaptation

Historical reconstructions are adapted for modern home cooking and are not presented as exact ancient recipes unless directly documented.

## Important

Nutrition values are estimates and can vary with ingredients and brands.

Supermarket prices and aisle guides in this prototype are demonstration data, not live quotations. Exact prices and aisle layouts vary by store and branch.
